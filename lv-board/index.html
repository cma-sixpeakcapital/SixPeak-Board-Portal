  • Cause. The city issued a stop-work notice on October 23 that halted demolition, over a protected oak tree on the site, after neighborhood opposition.

  • Exposure. The bond was issued October 12 on the $22.07M GMP. Krueger's $561,958 is owed in cash either way; the $155,409 CBI premium is paid. LV's mobilization costs to date are limited, and the superintendent assigned to Acama (Alex Rodriguez) has moved to other work, so there is no idle payroll. The six-month slip costs $428,651 of FY2027 net income and is already in the budget. Owner claims: Grady to confirm none have been raised.
  • Open decision. Keep the contract in place, or terminate it to release the bonding capacity. Not yet decided.
  • Who owns it. The clearance sits with the owner (HVN) and the city; Grady Lakamp owns LV's restart and staffing plan.
