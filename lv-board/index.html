No significant incidents this period

From the October 27 LV executive meeting.

07 · Krueger proposal

Pay Krueger's bonding fee as an 8% note over each job's life

Instead of paying the cash fee in a lump sum, LV would borrow it from Krueger at 8% and repay it as a fixed share of LV's fee on that job as the work is billed and paid. It starts with the $1,228,421 owed on Francis, Lexington and Acama (in place of the January 2027 payout) and applies to each future bond in the program. This is a proposal to Krueger; it is not in the approved budget.

Key terms

  • Rate and repayment. 8.00%, computed monthly. Each job's fixed Fee Share of the supervision, GC fee and admin billed is paid within 10 business days of LV being paid.
  • Maturity. 30 days after the job's final payment, or the scheduled final payment plus six months if earlier. Free prepayment at any time.
  • Stopped jobs. If a job has no fee billing for six months, LV pays the interest in cash until billing resumes.
  • Ranking and program. Unsecured, behind LV's obligations to the surety. No change to the 3.25% program fee or to Krueger's vested equity; no further equity is issued.
