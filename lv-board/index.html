Key assumptions

  • Plan. September-close Base Case (Beverly included from Oct 2027), with Reseda. The Base Case projection is this budget. Excludes Uplifters and BD placeholder deals.
  • Rollup. LV, Six Peak, and APA at Six Peak's 50% share. No intercompany charges.
  • Fee. 14% of the Cost of the Work (6% supervision, 6% fee, 2% admin). The model calculates it as 12.04% of the contract (GMP), because the GMP already includes the fee and other items. Same dollars.
  • Trade cost. Passed through at cost on LIHTC jobs. On Ramsgate and Nelrose, buyout savings are split 50/50 with the owner.
  • Bonding. Owner pays a 1.5% bond line (Reseda 2.0%). LV pays Krueger 3.25% and CBI about 0.73%. Bonds post at job start. Acama's bond was issued in Oct 2026.
  • LV payroll. Staffing engine: crew follows job starts, and current staff move to new jobs as jobs finish. PMs and APMs overlap jobs for a job's last 5 months. A role is hired only when jobs need at least 0.75 of a person. No field engineers. No 2027 raises. $200K year-end bonus pool each December ($75K paid for 2026).
  • Six Peak people. Partner consulting fees $800K; payroll $658K; consultants $337K. No 2027 raises except Robert Carrega, +5% from January.
  • Rent. LV $15K/mo. Six Peak $5K/mo, plus NYC office full rent (~$3.5K/mo) through Jan and half rent Feb-Apr, then none.
  • Pay-app automation. $95K: $47K setup plus $4K/mo license (rises to $5,550/mo in 2028).
  • BD commission. Tom Taggart, $63K on Beverly (Nov).
  • APA. No Services Fee in FY2027; Six Peak receives an $800 dividend.
  • January 2027. Tax distribution $246K and Krueger payout $1.23M (Francis, Lexington, Acama).
  • Steyn facility. $112K/mo from Aug 2027.
