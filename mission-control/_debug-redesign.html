The Scaling Mechanism

Add a project. Hire onsite staff. That's it.

Mission Control automates the non-site work, so a new project requires only a superintendent and a field engineer on the ground. PMs, coordinators, and accounting absorb the incremental back-office load through the platform — no new PM, no new coordinator, no added accounting staff for each project.

The Multiplier

The pipeline is the juice. Mission Control is the multiplier.

On top of the $19.4M baseline, the platform layers a margin-expansion and deferred-hiring overlay — cost-control automation, faster draws, and fewer change-order errors across the $480M of BD volume.
